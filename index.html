Privacy Policy for Celestial

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Celestial ("Celestial", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Celestial Academic Hub mobile application.

Celestial is an academic resources application developed by Md Ashhad Ali. The App provides students with access to academic resources, lecture notes, Previous Year Questions (PYQs), and other educational documents.

Privacy Summary: Celestial does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information. The App primarily communicates with its backend to retrieve academic resources and opens resource files for viewing or downloading.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

Celestial does not require users to register or create an account. The App does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

We do not intentionally collect or store:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal or physical address
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise location information
  • Microphone or audio information

1.2 Information Entered or Selected in the App

Celestial allows users to select academic information such as their program, branch, semester, and subject. Users can also search and filter available academic resources.

These selections and search queries are used to determine which academic resources should be displayed. The App does not require these selections to be associated with a user account or personal identity.

1.3 Network Information

Celestial communicates with its backend servers over the Internet to retrieve academic information and resource details.

As part of normal Internet communication, the servers handling these requests may receive technical network information, such as an IP address. This information is required for normal Internet communication and delivery of requested content.

Celestial does not intentionally use network information to identify individual users.

2. How Information Is Used

Information and network communication are used to provide the core functionality of Celestial, including:

  • Retrieving academic programs and branches
  • Retrieving semester and subject information
  • Retrieving available academic resources
  • Displaying lecture notes
  • Displaying Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
  • Searching and filtering academic resources
  • Opening academic documents
  • Allowing users to view or download available resources

Celestial does not use personal information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or targeted marketing.

3. Backend and Academic Resources

Celestial uses backend and hosting infrastructure to provide academic information and resource files.

The App communicates with its backend through Internet requests to retrieve information such as programs, branches, semesters, subjects, and available resources.

Academic documents may be hosted separately from the App itself. When a user chooses to view or download a document, Celestial opens the corresponding resource URL using an external browser or supported application on the device.

4. Third-Party Services

Celestial does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, or crash-reporting services.

The App uses standard Flutter packages for network communication and opening external URLs. These packages are used to provide the App's functionality and are not used by Celestial to create user profiles or track users.

The servers and hosting infrastructure used to provide academic resources may process technical information required to handle network requests.

When an academic resource is opened in an external browser or application, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Advertising

Celestial does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Celestial does not currently use third-party analytics services or tracking SDKs to monitor user behavior.

The App does not intentionally create advertising profiles, behavioral profiles, or cross-application tracking profiles.

7. Data Sharing

Celestial does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

The App does not intentionally share personal information with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Technical network information may be processed by the backend or hosting infrastructure when necessary to deliver requested academic resources and operate the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or a valid governmental request.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the systems used to operate Celestial and provide its academic resources.

Communication between the App and supported backend services is performed using HTTPS, helping protect information while it is transmitted over the Internet.

However, no method of Internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the Internet.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Celestial does not create or maintain user accounts and does not intentionally store personal information submitted by users.

Because the App does not provide user accounts or personal profiles, there is no personal account information that users need to delete.

Technical information that may be processed as part of normal Internet communication is handled according to the applicable practices of the backend and hosting infrastructure.

10. Children's Privacy

Celestial is an educational application intended for students and general academic use.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the contact information below.

11. External Websites and Applications

Celestial may open academic resource URLs in an external browser or another application installed on the user's device.

Celestial does not control the privacy practices of external websites, browsers, file-hosting services, or other third-party applications.

Any information processed by such third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the functionality, services, or privacy practices of Celestial change.

Any updated version will be published on this page, and the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
