Privacy Policy for Celestial

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Celestial (the "App"), developed by Ashhad Ali, handles information when you use the application.

Celestial is an academic resources application that provides students with access to lecture notes, study materials, Previous Year Questions (PYQs), and other educational resources in PDF format.

Privacy at a glance: Celestial does not require users to create an account or log in. The App does not intentionally collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

Celestial does not require you to provide personal information in order to use the App.

We do not intentionally collect or store:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal or physical address
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise location information

Technical Information

Celestial does not use analytics or advertising SDKs to collect information about users.

When the App connects to our servers to retrieve academic resources, normal Internet communication may cause technical information such as an IP address to be transmitted to the server. This is necessary for Internet communication and delivery of requested content.

Celestial does not intentionally use such technical information to identify individual users.

2. How We Use Information

Celestial uses network communication solely to provide the App's functionality, including:

  • Retrieving academic resources
  • Downloading PDF files
  • Displaying study materials within the App
  • Maintaining the availability of educational content

We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, behavioral tracking, or targeted marketing.

3. Academic Resources

Celestial provides academic resources such as lecture notes, Previous Year Questions (PYQs), and other educational materials.

When you request a resource, the App connects to our backend or hosting server to retrieve the requested file.

These resources are provided for educational purposes. The App does not require you to submit personal information to access them.

4. Third-Party Services

Celestial does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, or user-account services.

The App may use Internet hosting or server infrastructure to store and deliver academic resources. When the App communicates with these servers, normal network information may be transmitted as part of the connection.

Such infrastructure providers may have their own privacy policies governing their services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers, analytics companies, or data brokers.

Technical information may be processed by our hosting or server infrastructure as necessary to provide the requested academic resources and maintain the service.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

6. Advertising

Celestial does not currently display advertisements and does not use an advertising SDK.

7. Analytics and Tracking

Celestial does not currently use third-party analytics services or tracking technologies to monitor users' behavior across the App.

We do not intentionally create advertising profiles or behavioral profiles of users.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the systems used to provide Celestial and its academic resources.

Where supported, communication between the App and our servers is protected using HTTPS encryption.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the Internet.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Celestial does not maintain user accounts and does not intentionally store personal information submitted by users.

Because there are no user accounts or personal profiles in the App, there is no personal account data that users need to delete.

Technical information that may be processed temporarily as part of normal server communication is handled according to the applicable server and hosting infrastructure practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Celestial is an educational application intended for general academic use.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address provided below.

11. External Links

Celestial may contain links to external websites or resources.

If you follow a link to a third-party website, you will leave the Celestial application. These websites are not operated or controlled by us and may have their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website that you visit.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality, services, or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page, and the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly.
