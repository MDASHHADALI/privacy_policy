Privacy Policy for Celestial

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Celestial mobile application (the "App"), designed to provide academic resources, lecture notes, and Previous Year Questions (PYQs). This document explains how your information is handled when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Since the App operates without requiring user accounts or logins, we minimize the data we collect.

  • Personal Information: We do not explicitly require, collect, or store personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Usage Data & Device Information: When you access the App, certain data may be collected automatically by standard application frameworks or app stores (like Google Play Console). This may include your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device type, operating system version, the configuration of the app, and the time and date of your use.
  • Crash Reports: If the App crashes, it may automatically send an error report containing anonymous diagnostic data to help us fix the issue.

2. How We Use the Information

Any anonymous or automatically collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (fetching and displaying PDFs).
  • To monitor the usage and performance of the App.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or bugs.

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes third-party backend infrastructure to host the database and deliver PDF files. These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they handle network requests:

  • Cloud Hosting & Database Providers: We use external servers to store and serve the academic resources. When you download a file, your IP address makes a direct request to these servers.
  • Google Play Services: The App is distributed via the Google Play Store, which may collect analytics and crash data according to Google’s privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in using our App. While we do not collect personal data, the network requests made to fetch study materials are secured using standard encryption protocols (HTTPS). However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Links to Other Sites

The App may contain links to external websites (such as external reference materials). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites.

6. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
